Read `CORE.md` and `config.yaml` first. Never read `docs/_archive/` or old story folders. Follow `CORE.md` as the source of truth.

Follow CORE.md section 1 for enclosed textured sets, live R6 action, body-first framing, 1.2–2.2 second cuts and screen_capture visual QA. Reset the camera after each take. Observe the owner-gated recording and Studio hygiene rules in CORE.md.

For a scene built around an interruption, review the approach, interruption, stop, reaction, and exit in sequence at the actual portrait crop. For example, let the viewer see the protagonist moving before the second character enters. Hold an angle that shows whom the protagonist is watching and the distance between them. Check eyelines through the reaction and reserve a separate shot for the turn away. Use cuts motivated by action or dialogue instead of repeated zooms. Verify revised takes in recorded footage, where timing and framing may differ from the Studio preview.
