## Narration (165 words, 1st person, Revision 1) — also saved as narration.txt
In this beach town, the first time you're truly brave, your outline glows gold. Forever. Everyone glows. Even my little brother, Milo. I'm sixteen, and I've never lit up.
Then the storm came. A rip current dragged Milo out. He screamed my name. And I froze. Rosa, the brightest glow on the beach, swam past me and saved him.
I asked how she got so bright. "I didn't swim out. I was too scared. So I screamed for help until someone came." Her glow came while she was shaking.
A week later, a kid got swept out near the pier. Rosa was far off, looking away. I couldn't swim out there. But I could run. I sprinted down the pier and rang the shore bell. Rosa turned. I looked down. My hands were gold.
She swam. The kid was safe. Milo hugged me on the pier, both of us glowing, the bell still swinging. Brave isn't not being scared. It's being scared, and doing it anyway.

## Meaning
Earned through the mirror: Rosa's glow and Kai's both come from calling for help while too scared to go in, not from heroics. The freeze in act 2 makes the bell in act 4 count.
