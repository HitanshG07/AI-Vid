   - **Option A (Simultaneous Binge):** Post Part 1, immediately followed by Part 2 (1-5 minutes later), then pin the Part 2 link on Part 1's comment section. High retention scrollers will binge both back-to-back.
   - **Option B (Cliffhanger Build):** Post Part 1 at peak afternoon hours (e.g., 3:00 PM - 5:00 PM local time), let comments and anticipation build, then publish Part 2 around 2 hours later.
2. **Shorts / TikTok Features:**
   - Link Part 2 directly as a "Related Video" in YouTube Shorts Studio under the Part 1 settings.
   - Add both parts to a TikTok Playlist titled **"Tears to Glass Keys"**.
3. **Thumbnail Customization:**
