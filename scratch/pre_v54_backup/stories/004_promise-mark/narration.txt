In my world, every time you break a promise, a red dot appears. I promised Ben we'd practice his dance. Then the coach called my name. The school show tryouts had started. I'd waited all year for this. I figured Ben could wait too. By the wall, he waited alone. That's when the red dot popped. The coach stopped and looked up. That red dot over my head? Everyone at school could see it. Here, break a promise and pop. You get a dot like mine. I yelled sorry across the yard. Ben turned to leave without me. The dot stayed right where it was. Last call, the coach said. I could still take my turn. I stepped out of the line. Then I ran back to Ben. Can we start from the top? He said he'd forgotten the steps. So I counted while he tried. One, two. He missed the turn. We took it from the top. This time he made every step. Right then, my dot went out. Ben grinned. Again? he asked. This time, I stayed with him.
