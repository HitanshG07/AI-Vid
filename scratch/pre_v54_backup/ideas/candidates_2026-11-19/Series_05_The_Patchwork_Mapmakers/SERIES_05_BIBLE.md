# SERIES 05 BIBLE: "The Patchwork Mapmakers"

- **Series ID:** `SERIES_05_The_Patchwork_Mapmakers`
- **Format:** 5-Part Serialized Roblox R6 Shorts (`1080x1920`, `60 FPS`, `50–56s` per part, `230 WPM` gap-trimmed narration)
- **Core World Rule:** In Seamstress Hollow, every street, alley, and staircase physically slides and rearranges itself every sunrise—unless someone stitches and pins a handmade cloth patch onto the giant canvas Wall-Map in the town square to lock that block in place.

## 5-Part Visual Scene Summary (Scene 1–Scene 5)

- **Scene 1 (Part 1):** At four in the morning beneath the towering canvas Wall-Map, a silver seam-ripper slices clean through Grandma Mae's clinic patch just as the cobblestone streets behind Kaelen begin to grind sideways.
- **Scene 2 (Part 2):** As Grandma Mae's sage-roofed clinic slides into the swirling dawn mist of the Outer Maze, Kaelen and Zara corner white-coated courier Sable Vane clutching a fistful of freshly ripped cloth patches.
