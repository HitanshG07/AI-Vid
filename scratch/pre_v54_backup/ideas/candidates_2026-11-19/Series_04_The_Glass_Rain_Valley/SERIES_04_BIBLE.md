# SERIES 04 BIBLE: "Glass-Rain Valley"

- **Series ID:** `SERIES_04_The_Glass_Rain_Valley`
- **Format:** 5-Part Serialized Roblox R6 Shorts (`1080x1920`, `60 FPS`, `50–56s` per part, `230 WPM` gap-trimmed narration)
- **Core World Rule:** In Glass-Rain Valley, whenever a mountain storm strikes, raindrops freeze mid-air into clear glass marbles—each trapping the last honest wish spoken indoors before the thunderclap. Shattering a marble grants its wish for one full hour, unless the marble was stolen, in which case it shatters into jagged black shards.
- **Visual Style Lock:** Unified beveled blocky Roblox R6 silhouettes (`2.0x2.0x1.0` torso, `1.0x2.0x1.0` limbs, `1.2x1.2x1.2` head), `classic_v1` 3-layer eyes, zero grey-block placeholders, `WeldConstraint` held props, `SphereCast` camera occlusion guards, split-depth cliffhangers with zero fade-to-black.

## 5-Part Visual Scene Summary (Scene 1–Scene 5)

- **Scene 1 (Part 1):** Raindrops freeze into sparkling glass marbles above Juno's bakery roof as a rare cobalt-blue marble drops into her copper funnel—right as Vesper's black-gloved hand snatches the rim.
- **Scene 2 (Part 2):** Inside a rain-slicked cobble alley, Vesper smashes a fistful of stolen wish-marbles against the stone, erupting a jagged wall of black glass rain-shards directly across Juno and Ollie's path.

- **Scene 5 (Part 5):** Hector steps into the glass hail holding his rain-soaked notepad reading "I FORGAVE YOU FIVE YEARS AGO," turning the stolen cobalt marble warm blue as Vesper kneels and places it intact into Juno's palm.
