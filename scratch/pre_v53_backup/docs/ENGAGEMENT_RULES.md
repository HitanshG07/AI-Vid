# Engagement Rules (distilled from craftee_films, zaylento, noctaru.story, gneef.original, Flupzzy, Kyee, VoidCast)

## Non-negotiables: meaning + completeness
- Every story has ONE clear human meaning (sacrifice, honesty, grief, forgiveness, kindness...) that the events *prove*, not just state. No random filler, no "blabber" lines that don't move the story or the meaning.
- Every story is **complete**: setup → crisis → twist → resolution → meaning. No dangling threads, no abrupt end. Everything set up gets paid off.
- **Series are welcome** (2, 3 or more parts) when the story needs room. Each part is a clean ≤60s video; every part except the last ends on a cliffhanger, the final part fully resolves. The whole series is planned and scripted together before production, never "make up Part 2 later".
- **Length (v2 voice, 2026-11-19):** the default `viral_storyteller` voice (ChristopherNeural +18%, pauses compressed) runs ~184 words/min (measured, story 002) → **~185–210 words per 55–60s part**. Never go past that; split into parts instead.

## Structure per standalone (or across a series)
| Time | Beat | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1s | **Hook** | First sentence states the absurd rule or mystery. "In this town..." / "In this world..." / "My grandpa does X every day..." No intro, no name. |
| 1–10s | **Show the rule** | 2–3 quick visual consequences of the rule (everyday examples). |
| 10–20s | **Make it personal** | Narrator's unique disadvantage or secret (underdog: "I'm a zero"). |
| 20–40s | **Escalate** | Crisis with real stakes; each 5s gets worse. One clear ticking clock. |
| 40–52s | **Clever twist** | Solution uses the rule itself in an unexpected way — set up earlier, never random. |
| 52–60s | **Action-echo payoff** | The rule fires ONE last time to prove the meaning through action, plus a 2s karmic/comic sting (the bully, the mayor, the little brother). **Banned:** fortune-cookie moral lines ("Brave isn't...", "The lesson is..."). The meaning is shown, never lectured. |

Series: Part 1 = hook → rule → personal → escalation, ending at peak crisis with a question line. Middle parts open with a 3s recap and escalate. Final part opens with a 3s recap → twist → resolution → payoff. Times above stretch across the parts.

## Catchiness engine (v2, owner-locked 2026-11-19; from the 4M–8M view benchmarks)
1. **Active rule, not a badge.** The rule is a machine: *every time you do everyday thing X, physical thing Y happens*, ideally with a scale multiplier (small X = tiny Y, huge X = giant Y). Passive status markers (glows, labels that just sit there) are rejected.
2. **Rule visible in frame 1.** The first shot shows the rule happening (no establishing scenery).
3. **Playground (2–13s):** one absurd public disaster caused by the rule + one weird town adaptation + **3 planted clues** (concrete items/facts) that the climax will fire.
4. **Taboo broken on screen by 15–25s**, then a personal trap with a ticking clock.
5. **Rule of 3:** three escalating attempts using the planted clues, each fails harder; each lands on a spoken impact word ("Snap!", "No!") synced to an SFX cut.
6. **Rule-hack climax:** the hero wins by weaponising the rule's own mechanics (scale overload or a logical loophole), never by luck or an outside helper.
7. **Allowed shapes:** active-rule hack (above), or the dark-hook → wholesome 180° flip (scary rule/figure turns out kind). Both still need the 3 planted clues paid off.
8. **No lecture lines anywhere.** No adult explaining the moral.

## Pacing
- Cut / camera change every 1.2–2s (3s max, same as `docs/LESSONS_LEARNED.md`); something on screen changes every 2–4s.
- Sound-off: the world rule is visible on screen in every shot where it applies (AGENTS.md), so the video reads muted.
- SFX beat on every reveal (whoosh, ding, boing, crunch, thud).
- Short sentences. Spoken words only — no words the viewer must read.
- Face decal swaps on every emotional shift.
- Hashtags: 3–5 per platform (more reads as spam).
- Part 1 ends ON the endangered person (split-depth freeze), hard cut, no black.

## Scoring rubric (verifier scores each 1–10; avg ≥ 8.5 and none below 8 to produce)
- **Hook:** would a scroller stop in 1s? Is the rule instantly understood and weird?
- **Pacing:** no dead second; escalation is constant.
- **Twist:** surprising yet fair; fires the 3 planted clues; the climax hacks the rule itself.
- **Catchiness gates (each pass/fail):** active rule (not a badge) · rule visible in frame 1 · taboo broken by 25s · rule-of-3 attempts · action-echo ending with no moral line. Any FAIL = rewrite.
- **Payoff:** genuine emotion; would someone comment or share it?
- **Meaning & completeness (pass/fail gate):** is the meaning clear and earned by the events? Is every thread resolved with a real ending? Any filler line? FAIL here = rewrite regardless of scores.
Return the four scores, every gate result, and a one-sentence fix per score below 8.
