The gold cup spun by itself. Then it pointed at our janitor. Here, stolen things point straight home. They turn toward whoever lost them. Last week, my stolen bell found me. It stopped as soon as I touched it. Now everyone stared at Mr. Reed. Someone yelled, 'Check his cart!' He locked the wheels and stepped back. The cup kept right on turning. I followed its point past his cart. It swung as Miss May walked by. She stopped. The cup stopped too. She took one step to the left. The cup turned left with her. Then I saw a crack in the gold. A tiny red wing stuck out. Miss May leaned in. 'That's my bird.' 'I made it when I was ten.' 'Someone took it from my desk.' I pulled the cracked gold shell apart. A little clay bird fell into my hands. Even then, its beak turned toward her. I put the bird in her hands. The beak went still for good. Mr. Reed finally got his cart back. Miss May took her little bird home.
