# Verification — 011 The Glow You Get for Being Brave
- **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **World rule:** A person gains a permanent gold outline (glow) the first time they do something truly brave. Everyone in the beach town glows except the narrator, Kai (16).
- **Twist:** In a storm Kai freezes while his little brother is pulled out by a rip current. Lifeguard captain Rosa, the brightest glow in town, rescues him. She admits her own glow came the day she called for help because she was too scared to go in. At the next rescue Kai, terrified, does not swim. He runs and rings the shore bell nobody else noticed, and that is when he lights up.
- **Core meaning:** Courage is doing the right small thing while still scared, not being fearless or heroic.

## Verdict: PASS
Reason (verifier, mid tier): No story or video found shares the rule (permanent gold outline on first true bravery, narrator the lone unlit person) or the twist (lifeguard's glow came from calling for help, and the narrator lights up by ringing the bell while scared). Closest hits differ on trigger (anger, good deed, powers). Conditions to keep it distinct:
1. Keep the trigger as "brave while still scared". Never describe the glow as just "doing a good deed" (Lucy's Light).
2. Keep the glow permanent and community-wide, not an emotion display (Huny Town).
3. The bell and the lifeguard's confession carry the twist, so keep both.
Caveat: the YouTube Short and Lucy's Light pages could not be read, so judgment rests on their titles and snippets. The caveat is acceptable given the clearly different triggers.
