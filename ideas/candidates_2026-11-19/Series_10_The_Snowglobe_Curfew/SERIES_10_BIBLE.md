# SERIES 10 BIBLE — "The Snowglobe Curfew"

- **Format:** 5-Part Serialized Roblox 3D Story Shorts (`1080x1920`, `60 FPS`, `50–56s` per part, `230 WPM` gap-trimmed narration)
- **Core Premise:** In the alpine valley town of Frostpeak Hollow, a giant crystalline glass snowglobe dome seals the valley whenever someone tells a major lie—shrinking one full city block tighter every day at 6:00 AM until the town runs out of air.
- **Visual Standard:** Beveled blocky Roblox R6 (`2.0x2.0x1.0` torso, `1.0x2.0x1.0` limbs, `1.2x1.2x1.2` head), 3D layered hair, `classic_v1` 3-layer eye faces (white sclera, iris/pupil, 2 specular catchlights; zero `:|` smileys), `WeldConstraint` held props, `SphereCast` occlusion guard, split-depth cliffhangers (zero fade-to-black).

## 1-Sentence Visual Scene Descriptions (`Scene 1`–`Scene 5`)

- **Scene 1 (Part 1):** At 6:00 AM in Frostpeak Hollow, a towering crystalline glass dome slams down over the alpine valley, trapping swirling artificial snow as Nolan presses his frost-thermometer to the wall and realizes the dome is shrinking directly toward City Hall.
- **Scene 2 (Part 2):** On Day 2 after the shrinking glass shears off Baker Greta Voss's brick chimney, Nolan and Zara trace the glowing frost-fractures to a sealed bronze time-capsule under City Hall just as Mayor Henrik locks the iron gate from behind.
- **Scene 3 (Part 3):** On Day 3 with Deputy Jonah Pike's oxygen gauge at 38%, Mayor Henrik steps up to the town square microphone and confesses his twelve-year geothermal heater secret—only for the glass dome to groan and slam one block tighter instead of shattering.
- **Scene 4 (Part 4):** In the final hour at 9% oxygen after a pickaxe, propane torch, and three more confessions all fail, Nolan drops to one knee gasping and sees the glowing frost-fractures pointing straight at Mayor Henrik's daughter Lina frozen by the bell rope.
- **Scene 5 (Part 5):** Guided by Grandma Astrid's forgotten rule, Nolan hands Lina her unmailed architecture school letter in the bell tower so she and Henrik can finally speak honestly, shattering the giant glass dome into millions of weightless snowflakes over a breathing valley.
