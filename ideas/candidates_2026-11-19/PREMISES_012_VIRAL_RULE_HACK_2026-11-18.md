# Viral "Rule-Hack Engine" Premises for Story 012+ (2026-11-18)
*Every premise below is built on the 6-Law Rule-Hack Engine (`Active Input→Output Mechanic + Scale Multiplier` → `3-Beat Rule Playground + 3 Planted Ammo Clues` → `Rule-of-3 Failed Attempts` → `Genius Loophole / Scale-Overload Climax` → `Action Echo Punchline`).*

## P1 (Recommended for Story 012): "In This Town, Every Promise You Make Turns Into a Real Rope"
- **Hook (`0–2.5s`):** *"In this town, every promise you say out loud snaps a glowing rope between your wrist and theirs! Break the promise, and the rope yanks you straight through walls!"*
- **Active Input → Output + Scale Multiplier:**
  - *Input:* Saying *"I promise..."* out loud.
  - *Output:* A physical glowing rope ties your wrist to the other person's wrist until you fulfill it. Small promises make thin twine; life-or-death promises forge unbreakable steel cable. If you walk away or break it, the rope reels you in at `60 mph`!
- **3-Beat Rule Playground + 3 Planted Ammo Clues (`2.5–13.5s`):**
  1. *Public Disaster:* A kid promised his mom he'd be home by five—at `5:01`, *ZIIIP!*—he got yanked out of math class through a closed window!
  2. *Town Adaptation:* Politicians wear oven mitts over their mouths so they never accidentally say the P-word.
  3. *3 Planted Ammo Clues:* My clumsy best friend Leo owes me three silly promises he still hasn't kept:
     - **Clue 1 (String):** He promised to return my **borrowed pencil** (a thin yellow string).
     - **Clue 2 (Rope):** He promised to eat a **whole ghost pepper** at lunch (a thick red rope).
     - **Clue 3 (Chain):** He promised to help me repaint my **dad's garage** (a heavy iron chain).
- **Personal Trap + Crisis (`13.5–21s`):** During a winter field trip, the old wooden canyon bridge collapses! Leo's snow-bus slides over the cliff edge, dangling `100 feet` above the frozen river! The fire truck's winch is broken!
- **Rule-of-3 Escalating Failed Attempts (`21–39s`):**
  - I realize: if you break a promise, the rope reels the two of you together!
  - *Attempt 1 (Clue 1):* I snap the pencil in half so Leo can never return it—*PING!* The thin yellow string yanks the bus up two inches and *SNAPS!* Too weak!
  - *Attempt 2 (Clue 2):* I stomp the ghost pepper into the snow—*ZIIIP!* The red rope pulls the bus up five feet and *SHREDS* on the cliff rocks!
  - *Attempt 3 (Clue 3):* Even the iron garage-chain starts cracking as the bus bumper gives way!
- **Genius Rule-Hack Climax (`39–51s`):**
  - I can't make a promise *for* Leo—unless I make a promise to the whole town on the megaphone and immediately break it myself!
  - I tie my waist to the giant oak tree on the cliff, grab the fire chief's megaphone, look down at Leo, and shout: *"LEO! I PROMISE THE WHOLE TOWN I WILL NEVER LET GO OF YOUR HAND TODAY!"*—and then I open my palm!
  - *CLANG!* A glowing golden bridge-cable the thickness of a tree trunk erupts from my wrist to Leo's, instantly reeling the entire bus up the cliff face onto solid ground!
- **Action Echo Punchline (`51–56s`):**
  - On the snowbank, Leo coughs, hands me a half-chewed stub of my yellow pencil, and grins: *"Kept the first one."* *POOF!* The tiny yellow string vanishes—and the ghost-pepper rope immediately yanks his face into a snowdrift!

## P2: "In This Town, Your Pockets HoldWhatever People Think You Stole"
- **Hook (`0–2.5s`):** *"In this school, if someone accuses you of stealing something, a heavy iron copy of it instantly spawns inside your backpack! And you can't take it out until you prove you're innocent!"*
- **Active Input → Output + Scale Multiplier:**
  - *Input:* A false accusation pointed at you.
  - *Output:* A solid iron replica of the "stolen" item spawns in your bag. Accused of stealing a pen = `1 lb`; accused of stealing a car = `2,000 lbs` crushing you to the floor.
- **3-Beat Rule Playground + 3 Planted Ammo Clues (`2.5–13.5s`):**
  1. *Public Disaster:* A girl blamed her brother for eating her birthday cake—*THUD!*—a `40-pound` iron cake ripped through his shorts in the hallway!
  2. *Town Adaptation:* Everyone wears transparent mesh backpacks so nobody can point fingers.
  3. *3 Planted Clues:* The school bully, Marcus, frames me every week so I can barely walk:
     - **Clue 1:** An iron **baseball bat** (`15 lbs`).
     - **Clue 2:** An iron **golden trophy** (`30 lbs`).
     - **Clue 3:** Principal Vance's missing **master key** (`5 lbs`).
- **Crisis + Rule-of-3 Failed Attempts + Loophole Climax (`21–51s`):**
  - When the school boiler room floods and the steel fire-door jams shut with Marcus and five kids trapped inside, three crowbars bend (`Attempt 1`, `Attempt 2`, `Attempt 3`).
  - Cornered outside the glass window, I point at Marcus and shout through the vent: *"HEY EVERYONE! I SAW MARCUS STEAL THE CITY BULLDOZER!"*—Wait, no: I tell Marcus, *"ACCUSE ME OF STEALING THE WRECKING BALL OUTSIDE!"*
  - Marcus yells *"YOU STOLE THE WRECKING BALL!"*—*BOOM!* A `4-ton` iron wrecking ball spawns inside my backpack hanging on the fire-door handle, tearing the jammed steel door right off its hinges!
- **Action Echo Punchline (`51–56s`):**
  - As the kids rush out safe, Principal Vance spots his real master key falling out of Marcus's pocket—*CLANK!*—every iron weight in my bag vanishes and re-spawns inside Marcus's hoodie, pinning him flat to the tiles!

## P3: "In This Town, Every Compliment Makes You Float — Every Insult Makes You Heavy"
- **Hook (`0–2.5s`):** *"In this town, every compliment makes you float two feet higher, and every insult adds fifty pounds to your shoes!"*
- **Active Input → Output + Scale Multiplier:**
  - Sincere praise = upward buoyancy; sincere insult/roast = downward gravity weight.
- **3-Beat Rule Playground + 3 Planted Clues (`2.5–13.5s`):**
  1. *Public Disaster:* On Valentine's Day, the most popular boy in school got 80 compliments before first period and floated into the gym rafters—they had to poke him down with a broom!
  2. *Town Adaptation:* Mean teachers carry lead umbrellas, and nobody says *"Nice haircut"* outdoors on a windy day.
  3. *3 Planted Clues:* My older brother Sam and I roast each other every morning on the walk to school so our boots stay firmly on the ice (1. his crooked teeth, 2. his terrible cooking, 3. his squeaky laugh).
- **Crisis + Rule-of-3 Failed Attempts + Loophole Climax (`21–51s`):**
  - When the hillside cable-car snaps in a gale, Sam is blown off the platform, floating upward into the storm clouds because his crush just told him he was brave!
  - I grab his ankle, firing our 3 standard roasts (`Attempt 1: crooked teeth` -> slows him 2 feet; `Attempt 2: burnt pancakes` -> stops him mid-air, but the updraft pulls us both higher; `Attempt 3: squeaky laugh` -> doesn't work because we're both terrified!).
  - Realizing fake roasts don't add weight—only **100% honest complaints** work—I scream the one true complaint I've held in all year: *"SAM, YOU ALWAYS PRETEND YOU'RE FINE SO I WON'T WORRY, AND IT DRIVES ME CRAZY!"*
  - *THUD!* `500 pounds` of honest brotherly gravity drops us safely onto the padded roof of the snowplow!
- **Action Echo Punchline (`51–56s`):**
  - Lying on the roof, Sam wheezes, *"That was actually really smart, little bro"*—and *WHOOSH!* I immediately float three feet off the roof while he grabs my shoelace!
