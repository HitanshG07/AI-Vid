# SERIES 02 BIBLE: "The 11:59 Rust-Clock Train"

- **Series ID:** `SERIES_02_The_Rust_Clock_Train`
- **Format:** 5-Part Serialized Roblox R6 Shorts (`1080x1920`, `60 FPS`, `50–56s` per part, `230 WPM` gap-trimmed narration)
- **Core World Rule:** In Cinder Junction, a brass steam train stops at Platform 0 for 60 seconds every midnight; boarding it lets you relive one regret for a single minute, but stepping off leaves a permanent patch of living rust on your sleeve—and if the rust reaches your collar, you become part of the train's crew forever.
