- **Format:** 5-Part Serialized Roblox 3D Story Shorts (`1080x1920`, `60 FPS`, `50–56s` per part, `230 WPM` gap-trimmed narration)

## 1-Sentence Visual Scene Descriptions (`Scene 1`–`Scene 5`)

- **Scene 1 (Part 1):** Inside detention Room 204, Principal Mercer catches Devon and Chloe opening glowing chalk-doors, snatches the yellow chalk, tosses it inside a vault door labeled with his own name, and wipes off the chalk doorknob from the outside.
- **Scene 2 (Part 2):** Using a tiny yellow chalk stub from the tray, Devon draws a glowing peephole on the slate board and spots forty shredded scholarship portfolios inside Mercer's room just as the Principal's shadow approaches with a wet sponge.
- **Scene 3 (Part 3):** After art teacher Ms. Gomez reveals that courtyard limestone also activates the memory-slate board, Devon opens the back of Mercer's Truth Room and discovers honor-roll president Lucas Reed trapped inside clutching Devon's intact portfolio.
- **Scene 4 (Part 4):** Ten seconds before the 5:00 PM bell wipes the slate board automatically, every rescue attempt fails and the shrinking chalk door narrows to a glowing keyhole sealing Lucas in darkness as Principal Mercer enters with the master eraser.
- **Scene 5 (Part 5):** Instead of forcing a locked door, Devon uses dry limestone powder to draw an honest chalk window under his own name, reaches across the shared slate seam to hand Lucas a chalk nub, and pulls him out before Mercer quietly sets the yellow chalk back in the tray.
