# SERIES 03 BIBLE: "Echo-Bridge Academy"

- **Series ID:** `SERIES_03_The_Echo_Bridge_Academy`
- **Format:** 5-Part Serialized Roblox R6 Shorts (`1080x1920`, `60 FPS`, `50–56s` per part, `230 WPM` gap-trimmed narration)
- **Core World Rule:** At Echo-Bridge Academy—a cliffside island boarding school linked to the mainland by a single 100-plank timber suspension bridge—every broken promise made on campus snaps one plank into the canyon mist. When the 100th plank snaps, nobody can leave the island until the truth behind the biggest broken promise is spoken aloud and honored.
- **Visual Style Lock:** Unified beveled blocky Roblox R6 silhouettes (`2.0x2.0x1.0` torso, `1.0x2.0x1.0` limbs, `1.2x1.2x1.2` head), `classic_v1` 3-layer eyes, zero grey-block placeholders, `WeldConstraint` held props, `SphereCast` camera occlusion guards, split-depth cliffhangers with zero fade-to-black.

## 5-Part Visual Scene Summary (Scene 1–Scene 5)

- **Scene 1 (Part 1):** High above a fog-choked coastal canyon, thirteen oak planks snap off Echo-Bridge in a violent domino wave at midnight, leaving Plank 100 dangling by two trembling iron bolts.
- **Scene 2 (Part 2):** Beneath the groaning timber gatehouse, Sora and Priya shine a lantern onto Plank 100's fresh splinter—revealing a glowing carved academy scholarship seal burned deep into the wood grain.

- **Scene 5 (Part 5):** As Dean Holloway confesses the buried scholarship fund over the bell-tower mic and signs all thirteen grants live, one hundred golden-oak planks erupt across the canyon in a thunderous domino wave.
