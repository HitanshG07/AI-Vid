# SERIES 01 BIBLE: "The Paper-Lantern Debt"

- **Series ID:** `SERIES_01_The_Paper_Lantern_Town`
- **Format:** 5-Part Serialized Roblox R6 Shorts (`1080x1920`, `60 FPS`, `50–56s` per part, `230 WPM` gap-trimmed narration)
- **Core World Rule:** In Lantern Hollow, every time someone performs a secret kindness for another person, a glowing paper lantern floats into the giver's attic—and nobody can see who lit it until the annual Winter Blackout, when every electric light dies and the lanterns project the giver's shadow-name onto the snow.
- **Visual Style Lock:** Unified beveled blocky Roblox R6 silhouettes (`2.0x2.0x1.0` torso, `1.0x2.0x1.0` limbs, `1.2x1.2x1.2` head), `classic_v1` 3-layer eyes, zero grey-block placeholders, `WeldConstraint` held props, `SphereCast` camera occlusion guards, split-depth cliffhangers with zero fade-to-black.
