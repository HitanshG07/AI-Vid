## Narration (182 words, 1st person, r3, ~60 s) — also saved as narration.txt
In my town, every footstep glows with your body heat for sixty seconds. Walk, it's faint. Run, it blazes. Blind Striders hunt it.
Last week, a jogger sprinted to the bakery. Boom. Striders through the roof.
So we walk slow, in ice boots. Only the night van drives fast. My sister June hugs a hot-water bottle. The laundromat steams all night.
June saw her cat. She ran. Barefoot. Her prints blazed like fire. Sixty seconds. The Striders turned.
I threw ice boots. Hiss! Still bright.
I flung her bottle. Splat! It froze.
I dragged her into the laundromat. Crack! The door split. My prints led in.
I yanked the dryer open. Hot socks on. I stomped. Harder than ever. My trail blazed white, down Main Street, every Strider behind.
I jumped the night van. Socks onto the roof. They chased it onto the frozen lake. Crack! The ice melted. Every Strider sank.
The driver jumped out. Then he ran. One grey hand burst through the ice. Gone. Only his radio crackled.
My feet burned for weeks. So June carried me home. Slowly.

## Active rule mechanic (X -> Y, scale)
Every footstep (X) leaves a glowing heat print for 60 s (Y). Walk = faint orange, run = blazing orange-red, hot socks + stomp = white-hot (scale overload). Blind Striders track the brightest prints. Wheels leave no prints (the night van, planted beats 2 and 14).

2. **June's hot-water bottle** (beat 16) → attempt 2: thrown as decoy, "Splat!", freezes in snow, fails. Teaches: a decoy has to move and stay hot.
3. **Laundromat that steams all night** (beat 17, open dryer visible through the door) → attempt 3: hiding there fails ("Crack!"); then its hot socks power the climax.
