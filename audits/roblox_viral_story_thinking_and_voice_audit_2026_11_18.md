## 4. The Polished "Rule-Hack" Story-Thinking Pattern (Now Locked Into `docs/ENGAGEMENT_RULES.md`, `showrunner.md`, `verifier.md`, and `new-story/SKILL.md`)

Every story generated by your pipeline from now on must pass all **6 Laws** and **5 Hard Verifier Gates**:

1. **Law 1 — Active Toy/Trap Mechanic (`0.0–2.5s` Hook):**
   - Must follow the formula: *"In this [place], every time you [everyday action X], [physical object/consequence Y] immediately happens—and [Scale Multiplier Z: small X = tiny Y, huge X = giant Y]."*
2. **Law 2 — The 3-Beat Rule Playground (`2.5–13.5s`):**
   - **Beat A (Absurd Public Disaster):** One comedic or chaotic chain reaction at school/town (*e.g., 300 secret-mice chasing a girl through the cafeteria*).
   - **Beat B (Weird Town Adaptation):** One visual rule of survival (*e.g., the mayor taping his lips shut, or politicians wearing oven mitts*).
   - **Beat C (3 Planted "Chekhov's Ammo" Clues):** Three specific, concrete micro-facts or items planted casually in seconds `9–13.5s` that will be fired one by one in Act 3.
3. **Law 3 — Personal Trap + Ticking-Clock Crisis (`13.5–21.0s`):**
   - Establish why the narrator is uniquely disadvantaged by the rule, then hit a life-or-death crisis with an immediate countdown.
4. **Law 4 — The "Rule of 3" Escalating Failed Attempts (`21.0–39.0s`):**
   - The narrator fires **Planted Clue #1** → *Fails at step 10!*
   - Fires **Planted Clue #2** → *Fails at step 20!*
   - Fires **Planted Clue #3** → *Fails at the brink!*
   - Every attempt uses a spoken sound-effect trigger word (*"Poof!"*, *"Snap!"*, *"Clang!"*, *"No!"*) synced to an impact cut.
5. **Law 5 — The Genius Rule-Hack / Loophole Climax (`39.0–51.0s`):**
   - Cornered with zero normal options left, the narrator weaponizes the **exact mechanics of the rule itself**—either by confessing a massive 3-year emotional truth that overloads the scale multiplier ([R6Rants](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nn3_MOuxoho) pattern) or writing a 6-word logical loophole that tricks the system ([cannonanimates](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G3Xsw7HOrxc) pattern).
6. **Law 6 — The Action Echo Punchline (`51.0–56.0s` — Zero Preachy Moral Slogans):**
   - **BANNED:** Never end with a preachy moral summary (*"Brave isn't not being scared..."*).
   - **MANDATORY:** End on one final, funny or heartwarming trigger of the rule that proves the emotional takeaway through action.
